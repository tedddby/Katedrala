## Scope

The Katedrala landing page (`app/page.tsx`), a single static page. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

- Audience: developers, tech leads and architects of TypeScript/JavaScript codebases; secondary, builders of
  developer tools who want a machine-readable architecture model.
- Job: decide whether Katedrala is a serious engine worth exploring and watching.
- Action: explore how it works on the page, then open the repository (github.com/tedddby/Katedrala).
- Proof: Katedrala's real structure analysis of CodeVerse (644 files, 594 analyzed, 3,766 symbols, 14,233
  relationships, byte-identical across runs); a clearly labelled demonstration system for everything else.
- Constraints: every capability carries an honest status (available today vs in development for v1); no
  fabricated users, stars, benchmarks or customers; CodeVerse is a planned consumer, not a current one; reduced
  motion, keyboard access and a text alternative for every visualization; a deliberate mobile representation of
  the graph.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Load Path. Memorable moment: the demonstration system settles under gravity onto its foundation line, and testing
a member lights the load path it carries.

## Unresolved

Hosting target; there is no documentation site yet, so documentation links point at the repository's docs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a structural drawing of a codebase under load: dependencies are members, load flows down to the
foundations, and whatever carries the most is drawn heaviest. It refuses the dark developer-tool template (glowing
hero, screenshot, three feature cards) and any literal cathedral.

OWN-WORLD: A near-black drawing sheet (#0E1012) inked in chalk white (#E9E6DF) and graphite (#5D6166), with one
load blue (#3EA6FF) that lights only members carrying load right now. Sharp corners, hairline construction lines,
line weight proportional to measured load, hatched ground lines under foundations, condensed technical grotesk
headlines, monospace for every measured value.

STORY: Visitors watch structure emerge under load, test a member, see impact rise through everything resting on it,
read the evidence behind each number, and leave knowing Katedrala is a deterministic engine with a contract that is
honest about what ships today.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: two-line headline, one sentence, two actions. Right seven columns: the
demonstration system settles under gravity onto its foundation line, its drawing title block stating what it is and
what is still in development; hovering or focusing a member lights its load path and opens its identity card.

FORM: Load Path (graphic statics and Gaudí's hanging-chain models), position 1 of 7 on my ordered list, chosen by
the user as the pick card; seed key e2bab8cb.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
